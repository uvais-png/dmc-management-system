Sales

Sales executives · Trip desk › Sales
Hands off to Bookings
  1. 1Receive the enquiryThe agent or traveller’s WhatsApp message lands in Leads with the sender and number.
  2. 2Check the detailsDestination, dates, pax, child ages, nights, rooms and hotel categories are read and flagged High, Assumed or Missing.AI reads the message
  3. 3Generate the quotationPriced from rate data: hotels, transfers, tours, tickets, tax and markup, in your WhatsApp format. Missing ages? One question goes to the customer instead.
  4. 4Send or copy · R1Send on WhatsApp or copy the text. The sent version is saved as R1 with its prices.
  5. 5Revise when askedChange hotels, nights or pax and send again. Each change becomes R2, R3… and every version is kept.
  6. 6Customer confirmsPick the option the customer chose. Booking tasks are created for every hotel, transfer and ticket.

Bookings

Booking department · Trip desk › Bookings
Hands off to Accounts & Itinerary
  1. 1Tasks arriveOne task per hotel stay, one for transfers and tours, one for tickets, with dates, rooms and guests.
  2. 2Request suppliersCopy a ready supplier request for each task and send it to the hotel or transport company.
  3. 3Confirm each itemEnter the confirmation number. The progress bar fills as items are confirmed.
  4. !Not available? Return to salesMark it not available with a reason. The trip goes back to Sales with alternatives to offer.AI suggests alternatives
  5. 4Booking confirmedWhen every item is confirmed, send the customer a booking confirmation with the confirmation numbers.
The return loop, when a hotel isn’t available
Bookings marks Not available + reason › AI lists other hotels in that city › Sales sends revised quotation R2 › Customer confirms again › Bookings keeps items already confirmed, books the new one

Accounts

Accounts team · Trip desk › Accounts
Closes the trip file
  1. 1Proforma invoiceIssued against the confirmed quotation version, with advance % and due dates, sent on WhatsApp.
  2. 2Collect the advanceRecord the amount, mode and reference. The trip moves to Advance received.AI drafts reminders
  3. 3Approve trip expensesGuide expenses with receipt photos arrive against the trip for approval.AI flags unusual amounts
  4. 4Invoice & balanceCreate the invoice, send the payment request, record the balance. The trip is Paid.

Itinerary

Itinerary team · Trip desk › Itinerary
Hands off to guide & traveller
  1. 1Create the day scheduleBuilt from the confirmed quotation, with default times and a guide per city.
  2. 2Drag, drop & time itMove items between days, set times, add or remove items, assign a guide per day.AI checks clashes
  3. 3ShareSend the guide their days with an app link, and the traveller their day-wise plan, on WhatsApp.
  4. 4Track liveEvery tick, photo and expense from the guide shows in the live tracking feed.

Guide app & traveller

Guides on their phone · travellers on their phone
Feeds Itinerary & Accounts
  1. 1Tick and goThe guide ticks each item as it happens: transfer done, check-in done, tour done.
  2. 2Photos & notesAdd photos and notes on any item, straight from the phone camera.
  3. 3Expenses against the tripFuel, parking, tolls, tickets or meals, with a receipt photo, for Accounts to approve.
  4. 4Traveller follows alongThe traveller sees the day plan with each item marked done as the guide ticks it.

Where AI helps

Marked with this chip in the app

AI does the reading, checking and drafting; people decide and send. Nothing goes to a customer without a person pressing Send.

Reads every enquiryTurns template or free-text WhatsApp messages into a checked requirement sheet with a confidence score.
Asks one questionWhen a price-changing detail is missing, drafts a single combined question for the customer.
Suggests upsellsSpots add-ons that fit the trip, such as the Genting express cable car upgrade.
Finds alternativesWhen a hotel is not available, lists the other hotels in that city from your rate data.
Checks itinerariesFlags missing times, clashes and out-of-order items, and can arrange a day by time.
Drafts reminders & insightsWrites payment reminders, flags unusual expenses, and surfaces what needs attention on the dashboard.
