Design system

The Flyland DMC Services product system. Bright and board-first in the spirit of monday.com, built for one job: move every WhatsApp enquiry from New to Won in as few clicks as possible. Colourful status labels you change in one click, calm white work surfaces, clear numbers.

Brand

The Flyland DMC Services mark: an orbit of dots over a rounded wordmark. White on Flyland navy is the primary lockup and sits in the top bar of every screen. Use navy on white for light surfaces. Every list gets itSearch, Sl. No., quick filter, detailed filter, saved views, sort and pagination. Statements with a running balance (P&L, bank, agent statements) stay plain.
Sl. No. counts what you seeNumbers follow the current filter, sort and page: page 2 starts at 11. Grouped lists number in display order.
Search looks everywhereOne box searches every text column, including the grey sub-lines. Typing resets to page 1.
Quick filter: one clickChips come from the table’s status column with live counts. Click again or “All” to clear.
Detailed filter: per columnText columns get “contains”, numbers and money get from–to, dates get a date range, statuses get pick-many chips. The button shows how many are on.
Saved viewsName the current search, filters and sort and get it back in one click. Views live on the device; delete with ×.
Sort from the headerClick a column name: ascending ▲, again for descending ▼, a third time to clear. Money in different currencies sorts by its MYR value.
Pagination10 rows by default; 25 or 50 on request. The count always says what is shown and what was filtered out.
09

Interaction principles

One click to change statusStatus cells open the label picker in place. The change logs with time and user and confirms inline. No modal.
Everything filters everythingKPI tiles, battery segments, agent rows and owner avatars all filter the same table, and a chip shows what’s active with one-click clear.
Calm motion150–200 ms ease for colour and lift, 300 ms for bars. Hover lifts a tile 2 px; rows tint #F5F6F8.
Visible focus, real targets2 px blue ring with 2 px offset on every control. Nav and primary actions are at least 44 px; board rows 48–54 px.
Colour is never aloneStatuses carry their names, flags carry icons, chart series carry legends and tooltips.
Staff-only numbers stay hiddenCost, margin and margin % sit behind a switch and never reach the WhatsApp preview.
